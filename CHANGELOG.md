## 0.1.0 - playtest
- Main menu, seeds hidden.
- Election night: geographic state map with flip/recount markers, hover cards, state overview panels, county map with shaded, counted and outstanding bubble views, full county table.
- Campaign mode: candidate creator with political test, weekly strategy (message, geography, voter blocs, time, money), electorate gauge, media and social tabs, specific October surprises, hidden candidate traits.
- 2026 real candidates (Senate, governor, House) and real county data.
